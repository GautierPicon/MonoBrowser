## What's new in 1.5.0

### Ad blocking

- A native ad blocker is built in — no extension to install. Requests to known ad hosts (StevenBlack/hosts, ~76 000 unique domains, bundled with the app) are dropped before they reach the network
- Settings → Ad blocking shows the **Block ads** switch (on by default) and a counter of how many requests were blocked this session
- A second switch, **Hide ad elements (CSS)**, hides the empty placeholders blocked requests leave behind (ad slots, iframes from DoubleClick/googlesyndication, AdSense boxes). It is only shown while Block ads is enabled, since it has no effect otherwise
- The blocking list lives inside the app — no downloads, no updates, fully offline

### Trackpad gestures

- Swipe two fingers left or right on the trackpad to navigate back or forward — an arrow bubble slides in from the page edge, the same pattern Chrome uses
- Pages that scroll horizontally keep the gesture: the navigation only happens once the page can no longer scroll that way (and `overscroll-behavior-x: contain` is honoured)

### Audio

- Tabs playing sound show a speaker button in the tab strip; click it to mute or unmute
- `Ctrl+Shift+M` toggles the mute state of the current tab

### Appearance

- MonoBrowser now supports **System**, **Light and Dark** modes (Settings → Appearance); "System" follows macOS, including the built-in about pages

### Session restore

- The next launch reopens your window where you left it: tabs, current tab and window geometry (including maximized state) are all restored
- "Restore previous session" in Settings → Session turns it off if you prefer a clean start

### Favicons

- Favicons are applied as soon as the page loads, including SVG data URIs
- Sites that don't declare one are tried at `/favicon.ico`
- The built-in about pages now use the MonoBrowser monogram

### Fixes

- The cosmetic ad blocker actually works: the hiding stylesheet is now injected on every page load, and its invalid selector list (which made Chromium drop the whole rule)
- About pages no longer show the wrong background in dark mode

## Installation

1. Download the `MonoBrowser-1.5.0-macOS-arm64.zip` attached below
2. Unzip and drag `MonoBrowser.app` to `/Applications`
3. Launch it

> [!WARNING]
> The app is not code-signed (no Apple Developer account yet). On first launch macOS may refuse to open it ("developer cannot be verified"). Workaround: right-click → Open, or run `xattr -cr /Applications/MonoBrowser.app`.

## Requirements

- macOS on Apple Silicon
- No Python installation needed, everything is bundled
